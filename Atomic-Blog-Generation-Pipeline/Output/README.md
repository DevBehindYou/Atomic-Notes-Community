## Upload order and linking rule

Upload the Support Blogs in U-number order. The order follows the campaign weeks in `04_ATOMIC_NOTES_PROMOTION_PLAN.md` (L1, P1, S1, L3, P5, L5, P2, L2, S2, L4, P4, P3, S3, S4, S5), three Support Blogs per week.

- A Support Blog may link only to live Main Blogs and to Support Blogs with a lower U-number that are already uploaded.
- Its social posts go out only after it's live, and link to it, never to an unpublished blog.
- After each upload, paste the live URL into the table below and into that blog's posts in place of `{LINK}`.

```text
Support Blogs/
  U01 L1-S1 Local vs Cloud Storage/
    local-storage-vs-cloud-storage-notes.md     source of truth (passes validate + cluster-check)
    Medium article.docx                         paste into Medium
    Medium publishing notes.txt                 title, subtitle, tags, SEO, image order, steps
    01-banner.png, 02-..., 03-..., 04-...       banner and figures
    Plan.txt                                    keywords, platform, upload order, linking rule
    Super Support Posts/
      01 X/  02 LinkedIn/  03 Instagram/  04 Reddit/
      05 Threads/  06 Facebook/  07 Pinterest/  08 Bluesky/
        post.txt (copy) and notes.txt (image, alt text, timing, UTM link)
  U02 L1-S2 .../
```

## Rules

- Take every keyword from `keyword-registry.json`, and run `node scripts/cluster-check.mjs --draft <md>` before publishing.
- Each Support Blog links to its Main Blog. Each social post links to its Support Blog.
- Leave the canonical link empty on Medium, Substack, and DEV. Support Blogs are unique articles, not copies.
- Never call Atomic Notes open source (it's source-available), and don't use 'download and sign in' calls to action until the Google consent screen is published.
- Figures and social images: `python visuals/render_support.py <module> "Output/Support Blogs/<folder>"`, modules in `visuals/support/`.
- Keep folder and file names short. Word cannot open paths longer than about 259 characters.
