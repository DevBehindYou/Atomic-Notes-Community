🔍 Is open source software safer?

Not automatically. Public code means anyone can check how an app handles your data. It doesn't mean someone did.

What you can verify:
✅ What data leaves the app
✅ How the encryption works
✅ Which trackers are included

What it can't prove on its own:
❌ That the app on your phone was built from that code
