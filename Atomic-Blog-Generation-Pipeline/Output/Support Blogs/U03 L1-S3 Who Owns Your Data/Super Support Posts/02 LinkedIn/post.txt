"Your content remains yours." It's in almost every terms of service, and it's true. It's also the least useful sentence in the document.

Ownership in practice comes down to five questions:

1. Where does the file actually live?
2. What does the license you granted allow?
3. Can you export everything in a format other software reads?
4. What happens to your data if your account is locked or deleted?
5. What happens if the company shuts down?

GDPR Article 20 sets a legal floor for data portability in the EU, but it doesn't cover everything, and a self-serve export button matters more day to day.

I answered all five for my own app too, including the one where it only gets a partial score.

If you build products, which of these five would your users struggle with?
