Two big cloud outages in 2025 started deep inside the providers. AWS traced its October us-east-1 disruption to a race condition in DynamoDB's DNS management. Google traced its June outage to a policy check that crashed on a null pointer.

Neither had anything to do with notes. But every app built on those services could stall at once.

During a cloud outage, what happens to your notes depends on where the app keeps its main copy. Web-only apps go blank. Cache-first apps show what they cached. Local-first apps keep working and sync later.

I wrote up a 10-minute airplane mode test anyone can run on the notes app they rely on.
