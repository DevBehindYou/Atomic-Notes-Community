When people ask whether a notes app is secure, they usually mean "can someone read my notes?" That's the right instinct, aimed at the wrong place.

Most exposure happens around the app:

• the provider holding the encryption keys
• account takeover through reused passwords
• analytics SDKs and AI features sending data elsewhere
• sync metadata that reveals patterns
• old shared links, old signed-in devices, and stray backups

Verizon's 2025 DBIR put credential abuse at 22% of breaches as the leading initial access vector. That's the account, not the app.

For product teams: which of these does your security page actually address?
