I use AI.

I think AI is useful.

Atomic Notes still has no AI assistant reading your notes.

Those statements don't contradict each other.

AI creates real value in plenty of places. A private notebook can have a different purpose.

Sometimes you want somewhere to write:

• an unfinished idea
• a personal plan
• something embarrassing
• a difficult decision
• financial information
• a private reflection
• a draft you're not ready to share

In those moments, the absence of another processing layer is valuable in itself.

Atomic Notes isn't built on an anti AI philosophy.

It's built on user choice and data minimization.

A notebook doesn't need to become an AI product just because AI exists.

Sometimes the right feature is restraint.
