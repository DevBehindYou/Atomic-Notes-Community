Independent software gets weaker when it tries to copy every feature of the biggest product in its category.

Atomic Notes isn't trying to become:

• a team workspace
• a project management suite
• a database builder
• an AI writing platform
• a company intranet
• an everything app

It's trying to become a trustworthy notes app.

That sounds less ambitious.

In some ways, it's harder.

A simple tool has fewer places to hide poor decisions.

Speed matters.

Offline reliability matters.

Data ownership matters.

Search matters.

Sync matters.

Privacy matters.

The product has to do a small number of things extremely well.

That's the direction I want to keep following.
