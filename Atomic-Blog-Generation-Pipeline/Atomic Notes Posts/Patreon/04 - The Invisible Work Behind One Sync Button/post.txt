A button labeled Sync Now looks simple.

Behind it, the app may need to answer dozens of questions.

What happens if:

• the connection disappears halfway through?
• the app is killed right after the upload?
• the same request is retried twice?
• another device edited the same note?
• a note was deleted remotely?
• the cloud file disappeared?
• the Energy charge went through but the upload failed?
• you update the app in the middle of an unfinished sync?

Most people should never have to think about any of that.

That's the point.

A reliable product hides complexity by handling it correctly, not by pretending it doesn't exist.

A lot of my work on Atomic Notes happens in those failure paths. Every push carries a request id, so a retried sync runs once. Failed syncs refund their Energy. Colliding edits become a conflict copy instead of a silent overwrite.

None of it is visually exciting.

All of it matters.
