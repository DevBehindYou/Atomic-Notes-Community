When I started Atomic Notes, the obvious thing to focus on was the app itself.

But a real public project needs more than an APK.

It needs:

• documentation
• transparent releases
• testing
• migration plans
• security thinking
• community feedback
• infrastructure
• a place to report problems
• a place to explain architectural decisions
• sustainable development

That's why I'm also building the Atomic Notes Community website around the project.

The goal isn't just to ship versions.

It's to make the development process understandable and open to the people actually using the app.

If you follow the project here, test releases, report bugs, share feedback, or support the development, you're already part of that process. Supporters also get Atomic Coins early, as a thank-you.

Thank you. ⚛️
