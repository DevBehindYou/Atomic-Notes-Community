Independent software has a hard sustainability problem.

Servers cost money.

Domains cost money.

Databases cost money.

Testing costs money.

Distribution costs money.

Security work costs time.

The common answers are:

• subscriptions
• advertising
• aggressive upselling
• selling data

I wanted to try another model.

In Atomic Notes, writing and reading your notes never use Atomic Energy. Energy covers cloud sync only, because sync is what creates ongoing infrastructure costs. Every account gets 20 Energy a day for free.

The idea is to separate:

using your own notes

from

using hosted sync infrastructure.

I don't know yet whether Atomic Energy will be the final long-term model.

But I'd rather experiment in the open than quietly turn privacy into another subscription wall.

That's part of building Atomic Notes in public.
