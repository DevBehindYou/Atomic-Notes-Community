Cloud sync is useful.

Cloud dependency is something different.

Atomic Notes tries to keep the two apart.

Your notes live on your phone first.

If you choose to sync them, they go to a private folder in your own Google Drive.

The cloud helps move your notes between devices.

It doesn't need to be the reason your notes exist.

That distinction sits at the center of the project.

Your notes. Your storage. Your control. ⚛️
