Atomic Notes is built by one developer.

There's no giant company behind it.

No advertising network.

No growth team trying to maximize engagement.

No business model based on reading what people write.

That independence makes some things harder.

It also gives the project room to make different decisions.

Local first instead of cloud dependent.

User owned storage instead of total platform ownership.

Optional E2E instead of vague security claims.

Public code instead of "just trust us."

If you support Atomic Notes here, test the app, report a bug, share the project, or simply follow along, thank you.

You're helping prove that small software can still be built around user ownership.

Local first. Private by design. ⚛️
