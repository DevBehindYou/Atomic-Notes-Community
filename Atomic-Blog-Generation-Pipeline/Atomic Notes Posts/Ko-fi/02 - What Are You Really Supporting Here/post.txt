When someone supports Atomic Notes on Ko-fi, the money isn't buying access to their own notes.

Local note taking stays useful because the app is installed on your device, not because a recurring payment granted permission.

Support helps with everything around the project:

• development
• infrastructure
• testing
• domains
• security work
• releases
• maintenance
• community tools

I want Atomic Notes to have a sustainable future without ads, trackers, or selling anyone's attention.

That's what support here makes possible.
