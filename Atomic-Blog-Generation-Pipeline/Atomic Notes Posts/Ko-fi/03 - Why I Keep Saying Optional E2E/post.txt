You may notice that I describe Atomic Notes as having optional end to end encryption, instead of calling everything "encrypted."

That wording is on purpose.

Privacy claims should be precise.

Atomic Notes works without the encrypted vault, and you can turn on stronger protection whenever you want it.

With the vault on, a key made on your phone encrypts each note before it syncs.

With the vault off, HTTPS and your Google account protect your notes instead.

Different modes have different security properties.

I'd rather explain that clearly than use one broad security word for everything.

Good privacy software should help you understand what protection you actually have.
