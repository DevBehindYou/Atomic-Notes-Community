A notes app shouldn't become useless the moment your connection disappears.

Atomic Notes saves your notes locally first.

That means the core experience is built around your device, not around waiting for a server to answer.

You keep writing and working with your notes, then sync later when the connection comes back.

That sounds obvious.

But many modern apps work the other way around: the cloud sits at the center, and your device is just a temporary window into it.

Atomic Notes keeps the device important.

Local first isn't a backup feature. It's the foundation.
