Encryption gets most of the attention in privacy software.

It deserves attention.

But encryption is only one part of privacy.

Another question matters just as much:

How much information did the app collect in the first place?

Every unnecessary analytics event, identifier, behavioral profile and third-party SDK is one more thing you have to trust.

Atomic Notes is built around a simpler principle:

Collect what the system genuinely needs.

Avoid the rest.

Protecting stored data is half of privacy. Refusing unnecessary data is the other half.
