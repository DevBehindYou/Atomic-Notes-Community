Atomic Notes is deliberately not built around an AI assistant.

That doesn't mean AI is useless.

It means people should still have places where software does less.

A private notes app can be one of those places.

Write something.

Save it.

Find it later.

Sync it if you want.

Encrypt it if you want.

No AI needs to interpret the thought for the app to be useful.

Sometimes software earns trust by knowing when not to add another layer. ⚛️
