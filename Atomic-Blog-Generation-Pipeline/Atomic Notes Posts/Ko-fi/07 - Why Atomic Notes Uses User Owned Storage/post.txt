One question behind Atomic Notes was:

If your notes are yours, why should the app's cloud permanently own the files?

Atomic Notes syncs your notes into your own Google Drive.

The backend still does the necessary work: sign-in, sync coordination, version tracking and Atomic Energy. It never stores your note text.

But the storage model gives you a direct relationship with your synced note files.

It isn't full self-hosting.

It isn't device-only storage.

It's a middle path between convenience and ownership.

I think that's worth exploring.
