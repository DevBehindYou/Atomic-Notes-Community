You don't need Atomic Energy to write a note.

You don't need it to read your notes.

It isn't meant to turn your own information into a paywall.

Atomic Energy exists around cloud sync, because hosted infrastructure has a real cost. Every account gets 20 Energy a day for free, and a sync with nothing to upload costs nothing.

The idea is simple:

local notes stay local and usable

cloud operations use shared infrastructure

I'm still refining the system as Atomic Notes grows, and feedback on whether it feels fair is genuinely useful.

Building in public means the business model deserves scrutiny too.
